    43. The Renewed Covenant (Exodus 34: 5 - 10)

    The Starter

    Israelites committed idolatry by worshiping the golden calf. Instead of being offended by their sins to punish them, God renewed His covenant with Israelites. What was the renewed covenant? How could the renewed covenant help the Israelites to obey God? What's the purpose of that? As for us who are saved by grace, how could we learn from that and apply to our spiritual life?
