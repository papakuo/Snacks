    22. Mount Sinai (Exodus 19: 1 - 6)

    The Starter

    Mount Sinai is also called Mount Horeb. God revealed Himself to Moses in a burning bush at Mount Horeb. Now, God revealed Himself to Israelites at Mount Sinai to bring the Israelites to be His treasured possession. Both events had happened long time ago. What do they get to do with us believers who are saved by Jesus Christ, the Savior? Why do we need to learn about Mount Sinai?
