    27. The Laws for Justice and Sabbath (Exodus 23: 1 - 9)

    The Starter

    God is just and righteous. So, God commanded Israelites to be just by obeying His laws. God also commanded Israelites to keep the Sabbath to receive His blessings. So, for us who are saved by our savior Jesus Christ, should we obey the laws like the Israelites? Or, do we need to keep the Sabbath like the Israelites to be blessed with what God had promised them if they kept the Sabbath too? How do we understand them and apply them to our life?
