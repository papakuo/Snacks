    12. Conquests of Northern Kings (Joshua 11: 1 - 5)

    The Starter

    After seeing what had happened to the Amorite and southern kings of Canaan when they rebelled against the people of Israel, the northern kings of Canaan still wanted to rebel against the Israelites. With horses and chariots, it was not an ordinary army. Yet, God gave them into the hand of Israel. The Israelites not only killed them but hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire. Gibeonites were also mighty warriors. Why could they submit to the Israelites but not the rest of Canaanites? What could we learn from them?
