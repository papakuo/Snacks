    6. The Lords Army (Joshua 5: 13 - 15)

    The Starter

    After crossing the Red Sea out of Egypt, Moses led the people of Israel in the Sinai desert for 40 years. During that time, they were like a group of disobedient and rebellious people as God scolded them. After crossing the river Jordan, God called them the army of the Lord. So, how did they transform from one to the other? Also, to be the army of the Lord means that one could prevail in the world. Could this happen to us nowadays? If so, then how?
