    2. The Origin of Moses (Exodus 2: 5 - 10)

    The Starter

    Moses was an Israeli. Yet his name Moses was given by Pharaoh's daughter meaning that he was drowned out of the water. So, his name has connections to both Israelites and Egyptians. It seemed that God prepared Moses for both Israelites and gentiles from the beginning to fulfil a mission. So, how did God prepare Moses to fulfill the mission? What could we learn from Moses himself?
