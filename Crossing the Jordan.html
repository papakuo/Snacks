    3. Crossing the Jordan (Joshua 3: 2 - 8)

    The Starter

Actually, God was quite sincere about the people of Israel crossing the Jordan River. They needed to consecrate themselves. Then followed certain guidance for the crossing. Why was that? What should that affect the believers of Christ nowadays?
