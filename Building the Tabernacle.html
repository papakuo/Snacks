    45. Building the Tabernacle (Exodus 36: 1 - 7)

    The Starter

    After receiving more than enough offerings for the tabernacle, Bezalel and Oholiab and every craftsman in whom the Lord had put skill and intelligence to know how to do any work in the construction of the sanctuary should work in accordance with all that the Lord had commanded as described in the Bible. It seemed that they were the people credited for building the tabernacle. How about the rest of the community who made the offerings. What kind of credit did they get? As for us who are saved by grace, we made our offerings too. How would we get? How do we know our offerings were put in the right usage?
