    6. Making Bricks (Exodus 5: 3 - 9)

    The Starter

    Israelites used to be shepherds. After living in Egypt for many years, they were making bricks for Pharaoh. Not only had to work hard but they also lost their freedom. As believers, we also had to work in the world hard. Could we lose the control of our life like the Israelites, especially our spiritual life? If so, then what should we do?
