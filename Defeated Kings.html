    13. Defeated Kings (Joshua 12: 7 - 24)

    The Starter

    The people of Israel defeated two kings on the east side of Jordan and 31 kings on the west side of the Jordan. Even though those kings had strong fortresses, horses and chariots, the Bible didn't mention what kind of weapons the Israelites were having. They even hamstrung their enemy's horses and burned their chariots with fire as God commanded. So, all they had were foot soldiers and God's promise to deliver the enemies to their hands. If we were them, how do we know we could prevail in today's spiritual war fairs?
