    3. The Burning Bush (Exodus 3: 2 - 10)

    The Starter

    God could convey His messages to us through a dream, a sign, a word, etc. A burning bush might be just as phenomenal as the Yosemite Firefall in some people's mind. Yet, Moses knew it's a sign for him from God. A sign accompanied with God's messages that not only woke up his Israeli heart but also built a close connection with God. So, could we derive this kind of experience when God reveals His signs or words to us?
