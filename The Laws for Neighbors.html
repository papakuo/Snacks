    26. The Laws for Neighbors (Exodus 22: 21 - 27)

    The Starter

    Ever since God had announced the You Shall Not Covet Your Neighbor's House, Wife, etc. Commandment, people were wondering why the Commandment focused only on one's neighbor? Why is the neighbor's belonging so special? Who is one's neighbor? If we could understand the purpose of this Commandment then we would be able to understand and achieve what God has prepared for us. So, how do we understand God's intention for the Commandment?
