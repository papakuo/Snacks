    16. The Tenth Plague and Exodus (Exodus 12: 29 - 33)

    The Starter

    After the tenth plague, Pharaoh finally let the Israelites go. However, the Egyptians had lost their firstborns of men and animals. The Israelites had to leave their homeland to start an exodus journey. So, it seemed that nobody won. Yet, if we could understand God's plan, then we would know that it not only affected the Israelites but also the believers nowadays. So, how could we prevail under God's plan?
