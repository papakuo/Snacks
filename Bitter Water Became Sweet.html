    18. Bitter Water Became Sweet (Exodus 15: 22 - 27)

    The Starter

    Water is essential to life, especially in the desert. It could mean life and death. So, when the people of Israel couldn't find drinkable water because it's bitter, they panicked and complained. It's understandable. So, God told Moses to throw a log into the water. Then the bitter water became sweet. We are just like the Israelites. We often encounter bitter situations where we desperately need a solution. So, what should we do? Where could we find the log to turn the tide?
