    15. The Passover (Exodus 12: 7 - 13)

    The Starter

    Before striking the Egyptians with the final plague by killing their firstborns, God told Moses about the Passover. Even though the final plague would only be brought upon the Egyptians, the Israelites still needed to slay a blameless lamb and put its blood on the doorposts and the lintel of the house in order for God to pass over the house. Why was that? How do we apply that to our own life?
