    4. I Am Who I Am (Exodus 3: 13 - 17)

    The Starter

    When Moses asked for God's name, God replied "I Am", which meant the one and only and forever self-existence, a name that makes the demons shudder and gets the believers rewarded as described below.

      You believe that God is one; you do well. Even the demons believe—and shudder! (James 2: 19)

      for whoever would draw near to God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him. (Hebrews 11: 6)

    Yet, how much do we know about the name "I Am" to pursue it in life?
