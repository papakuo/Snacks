    33. The Most Holy Place (Exodus 26: 31 - 37)

    The Starter

    God is holy. So, the tabernacle that Israelites built for God's dwelling is holy too. Yet, what is holy? If we don't have a notion of holiness then we would not know how significant it was for God wanting to live in the midst of His people. Don't even mention what the Holy Place and the Most Holy Place in the tabernacle could do for us? Especially, the tabernacle along with the Holy Place and Most Holy Place were all gone. Does that mean the ark and the mercy seat would be all in the past for Israelites and the world?
