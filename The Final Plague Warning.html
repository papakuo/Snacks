    14. The Final Plague Warning (Exodus 11: 2 - 9)

    The Starter

    God told Moses to warn Pharaoh about the final plague of killing all of the firstborns in Egypt, men and cattle. God also told Moses to tell Israelite to ask their Egyptian neighbors for silver and gold jewelry because God gave the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians. It seemed to be impossible. How could God kill Egyptian firstborns like an enemy then ask Egyptians to provide their fortune to Israelites as a friend? Yet that's God's plan since the beginning. So, how do we understand what it means and how to apply it in life?
