    10. The Gibeonite (Joshua 9: 3 - 6)

    The Starter

    As commanded by God, the people of Israel struck down every person in the cities of Jericho and Ai, except Rahab and her family. That's because Rehab had made a deal with the Israeli spies to help them out of Jericho. The Gibeonite could have learned from Rehab. So, they pretended to be from far away that won't post any harm to the Israelites to trick the Israelites to make a covenant with them. After being found out later, the Israelites let them live as they promised to be the cutters of wood and drawers of water for all the congregation. Later on, the Bible showed that even God honored the covenant. So, what could we learn from the Gibeonite?
