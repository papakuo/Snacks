    5. Go Back to Egypt (Exodus 4: 19 - 23)

    The Starter

    Moses escaped from Egypt to Median because he was rejected by both Israelites, his native people, and Egyptians, the people he grew up with. Now, God called him out to go back to Egypt to bring the Israelites back to the land as God had promised. No wonder he was reluctant for the call. The same happens to us. After being saved, we are called by Christ to be his witness in the world all the time. If so, then what could we learn from how God had called Moses?
