    8. The Salvation (Exodus 7: 1 - 7)

    The Starter

    God told Moses to deliver Israel from Egypt, yet God would harden Pharaoh's heart to allow Israel to go. Therefore, those plagues. So, why was the trouble? If we could understand the rationale behind the plagues, then we'll know what God had planned not only for those generations but also the rest of the generations to come till the final days. Just as the Bible described below.

      For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans for welfare and not for evil, to give you a future and a hope. (Jeremiah 29: 11)

    So, how do we prepare our hearts for that?
