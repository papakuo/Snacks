The Allotment for Ephraim and Manasseh.

The allotment of the people of Joseph went from the Jordan by Jericho, east of the waters of Jericho, into the wilderness, going up from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel. Then going from Bethel to Luz, it passes along to Ataroth, the territory of the Archites. Then it goes down westward to the territory of the Japhletites, as far as the territory of Lower Beth-horon, then to Gezer, and it ends at the sea. The people of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance. (Joshua 16: 1 to 4)

Among the nine and a half Israeli tribes, Judah was the first tribe to inherit the allotment. The Ephraim and Manasseh tribes were the next. That's because of Jacob's prophecies to his sons. In his prophecies, Jacob forfeited Ruben's firstborn because of Reuben’s immorality with his father’s concubine Bilhah. Jacob also forfeited the rights of Simeon, the second born, and Levi, the third born because of their cruelty toward the men of Shechem. As for Judah, he also made mistakes. Yet, when faced with the life and death situation, he was willing to trade the place with Benjamin to spare Jacob's agony. So, Jacob praised him as the firstborn whom his brothers shall praise and the scepter shall not depart from him. As for Joseph, Jacob blessed him as below.

The blessings of your father are mighty beyond the blessings of my parents, up to the bounties of the everlasting hills. May they be on the head of Joseph, and on the brow of him who was set apart from his brothers. (Genesis 49: 26)

As the sons of Joseph, Ephraim and Manasseh were adopted by Jacob as described below.

And now your two sons, who were born to you in the land of Egypt before I came to you in Egypt, are mine; Ephraim and Manasseh shall be mine, as Reuben and Simeon are. And the children that you fathered after them shall be yours. They shall be called by the name of their brothers in their inheritance. (Genesis 48: 5 6)

That's why Judah, Ephraim and Manasseh inherited their allotments prior to the other tribes. Jacob's prophecies were made more than 450 years ago. Yet, it lasted even after so many years. No wonder God changed the name of Jacob to Israel. Because it's Jacob who decided the fate of Israel. The allotment indeed fulfilled the will of Jacob, Israel. As for Manasseh, who not only inherited vast land east of the Jordan, but also received a vast allotment on the west of the Jordan. Even some daughters of the tribe requested the land as described below.

They approached Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun and the leaders and said, “The Lord commanded Moses to give us an inheritance along with our brothers.” So according to the mouth of the Lord he gave them an inheritance among the brothers of their father. Thus there fell to Manasseh ten portions, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan, which is on the other side of the Jordan, because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance along with his sons. The land of Gilead was allotted to the rest of the people of Manasseh.(Joshua 17: 4 to 6)

Yet, compared to Manasseh, the allotment for Ephraim was much less. So, they requested Joshua for more land as below.

Then the people of Joseph spoke to Joshua, saying, “Why have you given me but one lot and one portion as an inheritance, although I am a numerous people, since all along the Lord has blessed me?” And Joshua said to them, “If you are a numerous people, go up by yourselves to the forest, and there clear ground for yourselves in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, since the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you.” (Joshua 17: 14 15)

When Jacob blessed Ephraim and Manasseh, he clearly blessed Ephraim more than Manasseh as below.

So he blessed them that day, saying, “By you Israel will pronounce blessings, saying, ‘God make you as Ephraim and as Manasseh.’” Thus he put Ephraim before Manasseh. (Genesis 48: 29)

Now, it seemed that the allotments made Manasseh greater than Ephraim. Yet, later on, Ephraim did become significant as the Bible referred Ephraim to the northern kingdom of Israel as described below.

The days of punishment have come; the days of recompense have come; Israel shall know it. The prophet is a fool; the man of the spirit is mad, because of your great iniquity and great hatred. The prophet is the watchman of Ephraim with my God; yet a fowler's snare is on all his ways, and hatred in the house of his God. (Hosea 9: 7 8)

Even though it became significant, it would still face the judgement. As for us who are saved by grace, we will also become significant as described below.

Jesus said to them, “Truly, I say to you, in the new world, when the Son of Man will sit on his glorious throne, you who have followed me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel. And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or lands, for my name's sake, will receive a hundredfold and will inherit eternal life. But many who are first will be last, and the last first. (Matthew 19: 28 to 30)

Not only significant, but also judging not being judged, inheriting not only life but eternal life. Immanuel.
