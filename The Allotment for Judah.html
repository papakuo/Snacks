    16. The Allotment for Judah (Joshua 15: 1 - 6)

    The Starter

    The allotment for the tribe of the people of Judah according to their clans reached southward to the boundary of Edom, to the wilderness of Zin at the farthest south. God also promised Caleb the land south of Canaan as his inheritance. It happened while the people of Israel were still wandering in the Sinai wilderness. Was it a coincidence that both inheritances were the same land? Or, was it because of Caleb? What could we learn from it?
