    35. The Court and Oil (Exodus 27: 9 - 13)

    The Starter

    The tabernacle that God commanded the people of Israel to build was not only for the Israelites but also for people who are saved by grace. Even for the ordinary and trivial objects, such as the court and the lampstand oil, they all carried important spiritual messages. So, how do we find out what the messages are? Or, how do we know the messages were not only applied to the Israelites but also applied to those who are saved by accepting Jesus Christ as our savior?
