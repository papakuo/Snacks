    20. The Lord is My Bannar (Exodus 17: 3 - 7)

    The Starter

    At Horeb, the people of Israel grumbled against Moses as before because they thirsted there for water. Then at Rephidim, Amalek came and fought with Israel. However, after the fight, Moses built an altar and called the name of it, The Lord Is My Banner, a hand upon the throne of the Lord! So, what had happened to change the attitude of the Israelites? From grumbling for water to fighting to Amalek without any grumbling? Could it happen to us? If so, then how do we ascertain?
