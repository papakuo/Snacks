    39. The Basin, Anointing Oil and Incense (Exodus 30: 18 - 21)

    The Starter

    Prior to commanding Moses to build the tabernacle, God had already shown Moses the pattern of the tabernacle on Mount Sinai. Now, even for the minor objects, such as the bronze basin, anointing oil and incense, God also instructed Moses how to make them in detail. So, how do we understand the spiritual meanings for them and apply them to our spiritual practice?
