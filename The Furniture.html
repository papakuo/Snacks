    31. The Furniture (Exodus 25: 10 - 16)

    The Starter

    God commanded the people of Israel to make a tabernacle so that He could live in their midst. In the tabernacle they needed to make an ark with a golden mercy seat, the bread of the Presence table and a golden lampstand in specific designs. Yet, the ark would be hidden behind a curtain so that only the High Priest could come near. So, it's not an easy access to God's mercy for the rest of the people. Now, they are all gone. Is there still a means for us to access God's mercy seat? If so, then how?
