    1. Male Children (Exodus 1: 18 - 22)

    The Starter

    The Bible describes God's love for Israel as "When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son." Yet "The more they were called, the more they went away; they kept sacrificing to the Baals and burning offerings to idols." Nonetheless, the journey of Exodus was the closest encounter of Israelites to God. So, the more we are familiar with Exodus, the more our eyes and mind should be opened to know about the awesome wonderful God. Yet, how do we prepare ourselves not to go away like Israelite afterward?
