    24. Laws And Restitution (Exodus 21: 1 - 6)

    The Starter

    After the Ten Commandments, God continued with the laws to solve the slavery, conflicts, restitution, etc. The question is that men are complicated because they all have their own judgements derived from their own deceitful knowledge of good and evil. So, will the laws be able to solve each case? Or, if neither party is satisfied then what's the solution? What's the purpose of the laws?
