    19. Manna (Exodus 16: 2 - 3)

    The Starter

    Manna, the food from heaven that fed the people of Israel during the 40 years of the exodus. Why don't we see manna anymore? If God could pour it down from heaven for 40 years then it shouldn't be a hard thing for God to do. So, why did God stop pouring down manna from heaven? Or, is God still doing that? If so, then how do we know, and how to find it?
