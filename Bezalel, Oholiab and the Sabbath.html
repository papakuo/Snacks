    40. Bezalel, Oholiab and the Sabbath (Exodus 31: 2 - 11)

    The Starter

    God formed Adam from the ground and breathed His Spirit into him to life and took him and put him in the garden of Eden to work it and keep it. Now God gave His Spirit to Bezalel and Oholiab so that they could make all of the necessary items for the tabernacle according to God's commands. God also commanded the people of Israel to observe the Sabbath. Other than serving God and obeying His commands, are there any further purpose and meanings from God? Especially for us who are saved by grace?
