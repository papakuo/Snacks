    23. The Ten Commandments (Exodus 20: 1 - 17)

    The Starter

    We all know or have heard about the Ten Commandments. Yet, men are so crooked and corrupt as described in the Bible below.

      The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately sick; who can understand it? (Jeremiah 17: 9)

    Could the Ten Commandments really help? For us who are saved by grace through Jesus Christ, do we still need to fulfill the Ten commandments as the Israelites? If so, then could we do a better job than them? If not then what's our way out?
