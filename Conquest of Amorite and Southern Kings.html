    11. Conquest of Amorite and Southern Kings (Joshua 10: 1 - 5)

    The Starter

    The people of Israel spared Rahab and Gibeonites even though God commanded the Israelites to strike down the Canaanites. However, instead of making a covenant with the Israelites like the Gibeonite, the kings of Amorite gathered their forces to make war against Gibeon. As pleaded by the Gibeonite, Joshua led the Israelites to fight against the Amorite kings. With God's help, all of the Amorite armies and kings were annihilated. Even under this circumstance, the southern Canaanite kings still made war against the Israelites. Why was that? What would we do instead?
