    37. The Consecration (Exodus 29: 1 - 9)

    The Starter

    To serve God as His priests, Aaron and his sons needed to be consecrated as God commanded. They needed to prepare some animals, wash themselves, wear the garments accordingly, and make several offerings to God for themselves. It should be a regular consecration throughout their generations. As for us who are saved by grace, the Bible says that we could also be God's priests. So, how could we be consecrated?
