    47. The Completion of the Tabernacle (Exodus 40: 1 - 9)

    The Starter

    God commanded the people of Israel to build the tabernacle so that He would live in their midst. Indeed, after the completion of the tabernacle, the glory of God filled the tabernacle and Moses was not able to enter the tent. Then the cloud of God was on the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of all the houses of Israel throughout all their journeys. As for us who are saved by grace nowadays, how should we build a tabernacle so that God would live in our midst? How do we find the cloud of God to lead us all our journeys?
