    21. Jethro (Exodus 18: 5 - 9)

    The Starter

    Jethro, a name we may not pay too much attention to. All we knew was that he was a priest of Median and the father-in-law of Moses. Yet, the more we understand about him, the more we know that he might have affected the Israeli people in a big way, even though he was not an Israeli. So, how could we learn about him to broaden our spiritual capacities?
