    15. The West of the Jordan Inheritance (Joshua 14: 2 - 5)

    The Starter

    The two and half Israeli tribes inherited the east of the Jordan land as they requested. The other nine and half tribes needed to inherit the west of the Jordan by lot. Could it be handled fairly between the tribes? Then Caleb was asking Joshua for Hebron. Without any objection from the tribes, Joshua blessed Caleb for Hebron to be his inheritance. So, could we imagine what would happen if it happened today? What could we learn from the attitude of the people of Israel?
