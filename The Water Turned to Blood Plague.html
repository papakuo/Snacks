    9. The Water Turned to Blood Plague (Exodus 7: 20 - 25)

    The Starter

    In order to break Pharaoh's will to let the Israelites go, God commanded Moses and Aaron to perform the first plague of turning all the water in Egypt to blood. Even though it did cause some disruptions in life, it didn't shake Pharaoh's mind. So, why did God do that? What could we learn the true meaning of the first plague?
